SFMx and SFMx Specifications
  POWER:
    Internal Power Features:
      * MPPT Charging System.
      * USB-C for Data and charging for setup.
      * 1 x 3350mAh Li-Ion 18650 (LiCB) 
        * = 4.20Volts: Fully Charged (100%).
        * < 3.68Volts: Operationally Discharged (0%); Instrument ceases to take measurements. >3.75Volts Instrument resumes to take measurements.
        * < 2.90Volts: Fully Discharged Battery; Instrument automatically switches off at and below this voltage when no external power connected.
      Charge Rate
      * Bus Plug Power as primary source:
        * 60mA – 200mA variable internal charge rate, maximum charge rate of 200mA is active when the external voltage rises above 16 Volts DC.
      * USB-C Power as primary source: 
        * 200mA fixed charge rate.

    External Power:
      * Bus Plug Power:	
        * 8-28.8Volts DC, via the Bus Plugs ONLY. Absolute MAX of 30Volts.
          * SFMx uses an internal DC-DC power supply for Bus Plug Power; Optimised for a direct Solar Panel connection without a MPPT or PWM solar controller in between.
            * non-polarised at the bus plugs.
              * 100mA maximum at 20 volts during charging.
              *  15mA minimum at 20 volts during idle, idle power conditions when battery fully charged.  
      * USB Power	Power: 
        * 5Volts DC. NOTE: Voltages above 5.5Volts will damage the SFM charging circuitry.
